# Interview and review modes

## Interview gate

Require a confirmed interview or an explicit request for full preparation. Locate the complete JD and recruiter updates, exact submitted resume or best-known submitted version, shared job context, interview time and round when known, and the current relevant complete career-vault records with approved facts.

If the submitted version is uncertain, state that risk and ask one concise question. Do not substitute the latest mother template without disclosure.

Load and follow the installed `prepare-interview-pack` Skill for the handbook, Battle Card, story coverage, question library, rehearsal plan, and validation. This workbench controls inputs, storage, and lifecycle only.

Pass existing company sources and unresolved identity conflicts into the interview skill. Every preparation mode must complete its basic company verification and research ledger before publication. Reuse earlier research only after checking name accuracy, source coverage, and freshness; a company heading or generic STARS map is not evidence that research happened. Store the ledger alongside the handbook and include `--company-research` in validation.

Save outputs under `面试/`. Put the most urgent rehearsal order and file links in chat; keep the full handbook in the folder.

## Review mode

Preserve supplied audio or transcript as a source file or source pointer. Do not claim audio analysis if only a partial transcript was available.

Extract questions asked, follow-up depth, challenged resume projects, answer strengths, unclear logic, unsupported wording, ownership drift, new role facts, interviewer priorities, objections, process updates, and proposed improvements.

Append the outcome and next action to the timeline. Draft a follow-up or thank-you message when requested, but do not send it.

Place new candidate claims in `待核验事实` with source, wording, metric boundary, and ownership. Use `career-proof` only after explicit approval. One interview result may justify answer refinement; it does not justify automatic resume strategy changes.
