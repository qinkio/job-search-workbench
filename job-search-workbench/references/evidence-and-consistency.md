# Evidence and cross-artifact consistency

## Source priority

Use evidence in this order:

1. Candidate-approved canonical claims with permission for the current purpose.
2. Candidate-confirmed facts with complete wording boundaries.
3. Current resume and formal project records.
4. Contemporaneous work assets.
5. Prior drafts only as leads, never as proof.

When the local career vault is available, read its current directory, relevant complete project cards and canonical claim register directly. Follow direct-vault-reading.md. No separately maintained bundle is required; choose only relevant, purpose-approved facts for generated artifacts. Exports are optional for actual transfers.

## Ownership vocabulary

- Use `负责` or `独立负责` only for work explicitly confirmed as independently owned.
- Use `与产品经理共同负责/共同推进` for co-owned PRD, metrics, launch decisions, reviews, and iteration when supported.
- Use `参与`, `参与推动`, `推进`, or `协同` for contributed work.
- Keep personal action, team result, inherited result, and project result distinct.

## Metric boundary

Do not use a metric unless its population, stage, time, meaning, and attribution are sufficiently clear for the exact sentence. If populations differ across stages, state stages separately rather than implying a same-cohort trend.

Use approved aggregates, ranges, or rounded values. Do not expose identifiable customer names, individual contract value, unit price, confidential revenue, or internal-only denominator unless explicitly cleared for that purpose.

## Scale and case boundaries

Keep each scale figure attached to its unit, time basis and ownership: cumulative personally operated merchants, concurrent merchants, system-supported merchants, covered staff, direct reports or stores in one case. These measures are not interchangeable. An individual case supports an achievement; its scope does not define the candidate’s entire role. Keep candidate-specific totals in the career vault or the scoped job record, not in reusable skill rules. New user corrections may inform the current draft, but do not silently update the career vault.

## One context, different expressions

All artifacts must point to the same evidence IDs or source facts. The greeting selects one or two strongest proofs; the resume expands the most relevant evidence; recruiter replies answer the exact question; interview preparation covers every project on the actually submitted resume; and interview review may create proposed facts but cannot silently change prior artifacts or the canonical vault.

Before delivery, compare role names, dates, project names, figures, populations, and ownership verbs across the greeting, submitted resume, job context, and interview scripts. Resolve conflicts or mark them `待核验`; never select the most favorable value automatically.


## New confirmations and substantive conflicts

Read the current role context and candidate supplements as well as the vault. An explicitly confirmed candidate fact with a traceable source and authorization for this application may be used locally before vault integration. Do not treat absence from the vault as disproof. Preserve source, confirmation date, intended use and wording boundary in the existing pending_facts record; distinguish confirmed local supplements from unconfirmed extracted proposals. A summary saying “confirmed” is evidence of a recorded confirmation, not independent verification of the underlying metric.

For a conflict, compare source, date, population, action, stage and measurement definition. A newer statement does not automatically supersede an older fact. Preserve substantive conflicts; resolve only with adequate evidence or a targeted clarification, continuing unaffected work. Do not silently update the vault.

For user/customer/store counts distinguish direct testers, their managed portfolio, data actually processed, deployment, ongoing activity and business improvement. Never infer one from another. Do not invent a more specific usage action when rewriting an ambiguous confirmation. Synthetic verification and real trials can coexist without proven business improvement.
